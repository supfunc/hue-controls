# Privacy Policy

*Hue Controls – Bluetooth. Last updated 11 October 2026.*

Hue Controls doesn't collect any data about you. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn't send anything over the internet.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Your lights.** When you add a light, the app saves the light's name and Bluetooth identifier on your Mac so it can find the light again. This never leaves your Mac. Removing a light, or deleting the app, removes it.
- **Bluetooth.** The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your Hue lights and control them.
- **Settings**, such as Open at Login, are stored on your Mac.

## Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are App Store purchases, handled entirely by Apple. The app checks with the App Store whether you've made them. The developer doesn't receive your name, email, payment details or any other personal information. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
