# Support

*Hue Controls – Bluetooth*

## Getting help

To report a problem or ask a question, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/supfunc/hue-controls/issues/new). Include your Mac model, your macOS version and the Hue light you're using.

## What you need

- A Mac with Bluetooth, running macOS 26 or later
- Philips Hue lights that have Bluetooth

## Adding a light

1. **Close the Hue app on your phone.** A light accepts only one Bluetooth connection at a time.
2. **Put the light in pairing mode.** A new light is already in pairing mode. For a light you've already set up, open the Hue app and go to **Settings → Voice assistants → Amazon Alexa** (or **Google Home**) **→ Make discoverable**.
3. Click the lightbulb in the menu bar, then click **Add** next to the light. If you've already added a light, click **Add Light…** first.
4. Accept when macOS asks to pair with the light.

## Common questions

**My light doesn't appear when I add it.**
Make sure the Hue app on your phone is closed and the light is in pairing mode (see above). Move the Mac closer to the light, and check that Bluetooth is on and Hue Controls is allowed in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth**.

**The controls are greyed out for a moment.**
The app connects only while its menu is open, and lets go a minute after you close it. This leaves your lights free for the Hue app on your phone. When you open the menu again, it takes a second or two to reconnect.

**The Hue app on my phone can't reach a light.**
A light accepts only one connection at a time. Close the Hue Controls menu and wait a minute, and the light will be free.

**How do I remove a light?**
Click the chevron next to the light's name, then click **Remove Light**.

**How do I stop the app opening at login?**
Turn off **Open at Login** in the panel, or in **System Settings → General → Login Items**.

**I bought the unlock on another Mac, or reinstalled the app.**
Open the unlock window from the bottom of the panel and click **Restore Purchase**, signed in with the same Apple Account.

**What's free?**
Switching lights on and off is always free. Brightness, colour temperature, colour and more than one light are free for 3 days, then unlocked with a one-time purchase. There's no subscription.
